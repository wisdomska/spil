How to play

A round takes about 15 minutes. You need 3 or more people and a phone each.

Start or join a room

The host taps Start a room, picks a pack and shares the link or the four character code. Everyone else taps Join with code. No account, just a nickname and a face.

Answer in private

About 50 Have you ever questions. Swipe right for yes, left for no, or use the buttons. Nobody sees your answers unless you switch on Spill Mode at the end.

Guess everyone's score

Your Spill Score runs from 0 to 100. The more you've done, the higher it goes. Slide to guess each friend's score. The closer you land, the more Mind Reader points you earn.

The reveal

The host starts the reveal. Scores pour in from lowest to highest, labels land, then the awards. Share your card to your Status before the group chat does it for you.

House rules

Laugh with people, not at them. If a question isn't right for your group, skip it.

Privacy

Short version: only your score is shared. Your answers stay yours.

What we collect

A nickname, the avatar you pick, your answers and your guesses, all tied to one room. No email, no phone number, no contacts.

Who sees what

Other players see your nickname, avatar, score, label and awards. Your yes and no answers are never shown unless you switch on Spill Mode, and then only to that room.

Question stats

We only show how many people said yes to a question when 3 or more people answered it, so no single answer points back to you.

How long we keep it

Rooms expire after the game. We keep anonymous counts to improve the packs.

Terms

The rules for using SPIL. Plain English, no traps.

Play fair

Don't use SPIL to bully, out or pressure anyone. Hosts can remove players, and we can close rooms that break these rules.

Nicknames

No impersonating real people, no slurs. We filter the obvious ones and you can report the rest.

Sharing

Your card is yours to post. Ask before you post someone else's.

It's a game

Scores are for fun. They say nothing official about anyone.

Contact

Found a bug, a bad question or a better idea? Tell us. To report a question mid game, use the flag on the card.
