# SPIL launch copy

Ready-to-paste text for listings and posts. Every claim here matches the live site
(https://spil-party.vercel.app/faq and /llms.txt). If a feature changes, update this file too.

Facts to keep consistent everywhere:

- Name: **SPIL**. Link: https://spil-party.vercel.app
- Maker: Senya (https://wisdomska.site)
- Ten party games, free, in the browser on everyone's own phone. No app, no signup.
- One person starts a room and shares a link or a four character code. Up to 12 players.
- Ages 13 and older. 18+ games and packs only appear when everyone in the room is 18 or older.
- Answers in the classic game stay private; only scores, labels and awards are shown.
- A round takes about 15 minutes. Rematch keeps the same room.
- Today's Spill: one question a day for everyone, with a streak.
- Accounts are optional (username and password) and only keep badges and crews across phones.

Do not claim: download counts, user numbers, reviews, a TV mode, or an app store app.

**First comment (from the maker):**
Hi, I'm Senya, and I made SPIL.

Most party games need one screen everyone stares at, or an app nobody wants to install. SPIL
runs in the browser on each person's own phone. Someone taps Start a game, shares the link or
the four character code, and everyone is in within seconds.

The classic game, Spill Score, is "Have you ever" with a twist: your answers stay private, and
the fun is guessing each other's score before the reveal. There are nine more: Most Likely To,
Hot Takes, Two Truths and a Spill, Imposter, Who Said That?, Rank the Room, Red Flag Green Flag,
Room Percentage and Spill or Skip.

It's free, it's for ages 13 and up, and 18+ packs only show when everyone in the room is 18 or
older. I'd love to hear which game your group ends up playing most.
